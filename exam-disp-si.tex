\noindent\textbf{Notes:}

Regarding the three questions on consistency, asymptotic normality, and the Cramér-Rao
inequality: To save time, you are welcome to display the main result on a slide before
beginning the proof. If you choose to display a slide, you must use the corresponding
PDF file.
\bigskip

Keep in mind that we have discussed several topics that are only partially covered by the main
questions, such as Wald tests, confidence intervals, the bootstrap, GEEs, nonparametric tests, and
causality, particularly the counterfactual model. You should be prepared to discuss these topics
after presenting the main question.
\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Exam:}

Prepare a presentation (12-14 minutes) of each of the main questions. After the main
question we will discuss topics related to the curriculum (approx. 10-12 minutes). Please prepare
your presentation in English. The first student draws their question at 8:30, or at 8:00 if they
have extended preparation time.
